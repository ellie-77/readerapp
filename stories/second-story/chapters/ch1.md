Nobody in the village could remember who had built the bell tower, only that it rang on its own once a year, always at dawn, always in the last week of autumn.

Teo had heard it eleven times. This year he decided to be underneath it when it happened.

* * *

He left the house before the lamps were lit, boots in hand so the stairs would not creak, and walked the long way round by the river so the dogs would not bark. The tower stood at the far end of the square, black against a sky that had not yet decided on a color.

He sat down on the cold step and waited.
